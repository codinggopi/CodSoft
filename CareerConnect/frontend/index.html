Frequently Asked Questions

Is CareerConnect free for candidates?

Yes! CareerConnect is completely free for job seekers. You can create a profile, upload your resume, and apply to unlimited jobs at no cost.

How do employers post jobs?

Employers can register for an Employer account, which grants access to a dedicated dashboard to post and manage job listings and review applications.

Can I update my uploaded resume?

Yes, you can manage your resumes through the "My Resume" section in your candidate dropdown menu. You can replace or delete your files anytime.
